How leave is counted

  • Only working days count. Weekends and public holidays are skipped.
  • Your manager approves first. Leave of more than 5 working days also needs HR approval.
  • Days come off your balance once the request is fully approved. Cancelling approved leave returns them.
  • Sick leave can be recorded up to 7 days after the fact. Other leave must be for today or later.
